Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

CardPilot ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Email address and name
  • Payment information (processed by PayPal; we do not store payment card details)
  • eBay tokens you authorize (stored to enable listing features)
  • Marketplace posts (wants, for-sale listings, and reported sales)

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, process subscriptions, enable marketplace matching, track reported sales for fee purposes, and communicate with you.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share:

  • Email addresses between marketplace users when you contact each other (as part of the matching service)
  • Information with service providers (e.g., hosting) as necessary to operate our services
  • Information when required by law or to protect our rights

4. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You use our services at your own risk.

5. Your Choices

You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us. Deleting your account may not remove data we are required to retain.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use our Contact page.
