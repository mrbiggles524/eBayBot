How to List Your Cards

  1. Find a checklist of the cards you want to list (Beckett, Cardsmiths Breaks, or Cardboard Connection).
  2. Copy the URL and paste it in Step 1. Set default quantities, then click Fetch Checklist.
  3. Listing details (set name, description) auto-fill. Input quantities for each card you want to sell.
  4. Fetch images or prices automatically for your cards with one click.
  5. Connect your eBay token and select payment, shipping, and return policies.
  6. Publish to eBay when ready.
