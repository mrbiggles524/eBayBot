How to Print a Label After a Sale

  1. Go to My eBay → Sold (or use the link above)
  2. Find the order and click Print shipping label
  3. Choose Standard Envelope (trading cards) if offered
  4. Pay with PayPal or your linked payment method
  5. Print and attach to your envelope
Tip: Use a top loader or cardboard in a rigid envelope so the card doesn’t bend. Standard Envelope works best for 1–3 cards.

Other Options

  • USPS Ground Advantage: ~$4–6 tracked (use Pirate Ship or USPS Click-N-Ship above)
  • PWE (Plain White Envelope): Ship yourself with a stamp (~$0.68), but no tracking

For most low-value cards, eBay Standard Envelope is the best balance of cost and seller protection.
